Let's do this
  • Press PLAY to start the music and visuals
  • Press LIBRARY to see more music and visuals
  • Press FX and turn the knobs for trippy fun
  • Press the Music Understanding tiles to jump around
  • Press the orientation switch to see square and portrait
  • Press record to output a recording of your session
  • Select a Music Understanding chip first and render a section
  • Drag and drop your own music, music analysis, and shader files
Vizzie is a techno rave love shader generator.
Vizzie uses music information generated from the Apple Music Understanding API using the SOZO App. This results in 12 parameters driving the music reactivity of the visuals.
